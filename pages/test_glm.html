About — the why behind the work

/about

I'd rather understand how a system fails before I decide how to build it.

I'm a third-year CS student who ends up on the backend by instinct. Most of my work sits somewhere between distributed systems and applied AI: producer-consumer queues, multi-agent orchestration, RAG pipelines, and the pipelines that deploy them.

I tend to say yes to things I haven't done before and figure out the rest on the way. That's how I ended up owning CI/CD for an 8-module microservices repo with zero prior experience — and shipping a matrix build strategy nobody asked for.

When I'm not building, I'm usually taking something apart to see how it works — a queue protocol, an agent loop, or the social mechanics behind a phishing scam targeting senior citizens.
